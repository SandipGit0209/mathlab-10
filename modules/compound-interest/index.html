Understand the mechanism

Interest earns interest

Simple interest

The principal remains unchanged in every year.

SI = PTR / 100

Compound interest

After a compounding period, interest is added to principal. The new amount becomes the next principal.

CI = CA − P

Compounded annually

Interest is added once each year.

CA = P(1 + R/100)T

Half-yearly

The annual rate is halved and the number of periods is doubled.

CA = P(1 + R/200)2T

Quarterly / terminally

The annual rate is divided by four and the number of periods is four times the years.

CA = P(1 + R/400)4T

T years and M months

Complete years compound; the remaining months are treated for the extra fraction shown in the book.

CA = P(1 + R/100)T(1 + MR/1200)

Different rate each year

Multiply one growth factor for each year's rate.

CA = P(1+R₁/100)(1+R₂/100)…
