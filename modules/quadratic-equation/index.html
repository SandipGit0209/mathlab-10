Quadratic equation

A second-degree equation with a ≠ 0.

ax²+bx+c=0

Discriminant

Determines the nature of the roots.

D=b²−4ac

Quadratic formula

x=(-b±√D)/(2a)

D > 0

Two distinct real roots.

D = 0

Equal real roots.

D < 0

No real roots.
