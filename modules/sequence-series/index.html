Patterns

Arithmetic and geometric sequences

Arithmetic sequence

Consecutive terms have a constant difference d.

tₙ=a+(n−1)d

Arithmetic series

Sum of first n terms.

Sₙ=n/2[2a+(n−1)d]

Arithmetic mean

One mean between a and b is their average.

m=(a+b)/2

Geometric sequence

Consecutive terms have a constant ratio r.

tₙ=arⁿ⁻¹

Geometric series

For r ≠ 1:

Sₙ=a(rⁿ−1)/(r−1)

Geometric mean

For positive a and b:

m=√(ab)
