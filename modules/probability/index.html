Chance

Theory, experiment and tree diagrams

Theoretical probability

P(E)=favourable outcomes / total outcomes

Experimental probability

frequency of E / number of trials

Range

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

Tree diagram

Each branch represents a possible outcome. Multiply probabilities along a path.

P(A then B)=P(A)×P(B|A)

With replacement

The selected object is returned before the next draw, so the composition and branch probabilities stay unchanged.

Without replacement

The selected object is not returned. The total decreases by one and the second-stage branch probabilities change.

Standard deck

A standard deck has 52 cards: 4 suits × 13 cards.

52 = 4 × 13

Red and black cards

Hearts and diamonds are red; clubs and spades are black.

26 red + 26 black

Face cards

J, Q and K are face cards. There are 3 per suit, so 12 face cards in total.

12 = 3 × 4
